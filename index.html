Run 2041, WhatsApp, airport pickup
What the agent did
Received at 09:12 on the business WhatsApp number, from a customer with two earlier bookings.
Read the customer's message: a pickup at Marrakech airport, Friday 14:30, 5 passengers.
Checked the fleet calendar. Two cars are free at that time, and the 7-seater fits the party.
Looked up the rate card and quoted 450 MAD for the transfer.
Added 25 minutes of waiting time, the house rule for airport pickups.
Drafted the reply in French, the language of the customer's message.
Prepared the booking record: customer, flight time, vehicle and price. It is not saved yet.
Waiting for approval. Nothing is sent and nothing is saved until a person says yes.
If approved: send the reply, create the booking and add the pickup to the driver's calendar.
If rejected: keep the draft, log the reason and hand the chat to a person.
Sources kept with the run: the message, the calendar view and the rate card row.
After approval, a reminder goes to the customer on Thursday at 18:00.
